Quality Glass Services, Backed by Over 25 Years of Experience

At GRN Glass, we provide professional glass services in Walkerton, Ontario and the surrounding area. Owned and operated by Jonah Misener, GRN Glass brings over 25 years of experience working with both commercial and residential clients.

From custom installations to repairs, we provide quality glass solutions designed to fit your needs. Our services include:

  • Frameless glass showers
  • Glass railings
  • Commercial storefronts
  • Custom-cut glass and mirrors
  • Screen repairs

With decades of hands-on experience, we take pride in quality workmanship, attention to detail, and reliable service.

Services

Frameless Showers

Give your bathroom a clean, modern look with a frameless glass shower door. It creates an open, spacious feel while showcasing your tile and shower design without bulky frames getting in the way.

Glass Railings

Add a modern, open feel to your home or business with custom glass railings. Durable and stylish, they provide safety without blocking your view or natural light.

Storefronts

Make a great first impression with a custom glass storefront designed for your business. Clean, professional, and built to let natural light in while showcasing your space.

Custom Cut Glass and Mirrors

Get the perfect fit for your space with custom-cut mirrors and glass. Whether it’s a unique shape, replacement pane, or finishing touch, we’ll cut it to your exact needs.

Screen Repairs

Keep your windows and doors looking their best with professional screen repairs. We can repair damaged screens and help get them back to working properly.

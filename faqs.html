Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about glass services in Walkerton and the surrounding area?
Here are some common questions about the services offered by GRN Glass.

What areas does GRN Glass serve?

GRN Glass is based in Walkerton, Ontario and provides glass services in Walkerton and the surrounding area.

What glass services do you offer?

GRN Glass provides a variety of residential and commercial glass services, including frameless glass showers, glass railings, commercial storefronts, custom-cut glass and mirrors, and screen repairs.

Do you install frameless glass showers?

Yes. GRN Glass provides frameless glass shower installations designed to give bathrooms a clean, modern, and open appearance.

Can you make custom-cut glass and mirrors?

Yes. GRN Glass offers custom-cut glass and mirrors for a variety of applications. Contact us to discuss the size, shape, and requirements of your project.

Do you repair window and door screens?

Yes. GRN Glass provides screen repair services for damaged screens on windows and doors.

Do you work on commercial projects?

Yes. GRN Glass works with commercial clients and provides services including custom glass storefronts.

How much does a glass project cost?

The cost depends on the type, size, materials, and requirements of your project. Contact GRN Glass to discuss your project and get more information.

How do I get a quote?

Contact GRN Glass to discuss your project. We can talk through what you need and determine the next steps for your project.

How much experience does GRN Glass have?

GRN Glass is owned and operated by Jonah Misener, who brings over 25 years of experience working with residential and commercial clients.

How can I contact GRN Glass?

Use the Contact button on the website to get in touch with GRN Glass about your project, service needs, or quote.
